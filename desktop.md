# Desktop Environment

ShapeBit Desktop is the custom Wayland desktop environment for ShapeBit OS. It
organizes work around persistent activities and materials rather than a taskbar
full of applications.

These pages describe intended behavior. They do not prove that the desktop has
been implemented or verified.
