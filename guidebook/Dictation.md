# Dictation

Dictation writes what you say into the app you're using. Speech is
recognized on your computer, in any of 25 languages; nothing you say leaves
it.

Dictation needs the **Dictation** extra, which Welcome offers. Until it's
downloaded, the dictation button leads to the download.

## Dictating

1. Click a text field, then press Super+D or click the dictation button. Or
   hold Super+D while you speak.
2. Speak. Each phrase appears in the field, as a draft, when you pause.
3. Press Super+D or click the button again to stop.

The words go into the field only if it still has the focus. They are always
copied too, so they're never lost. Use the app's own undo to take them back.

To only copy, without typing, right-click the button or press Super+Shift+D.

## Punctuation

Say punctuation as a word: "comma", "full stop", "question mark",
"exclamation mark", "colon", "semicolon", or in Polish "przecinek", "kropka",
"znak zapytania", "wykrzyknik", "dwukropek", "średnik".

## Tidy up

For a few seconds after dictated text goes in, a **Tidy up** button by the
dictation button offers to fix its punctuation, capitals, and misheard words,
keeping what you said. Super+T does the same until your next dictation. It
uses the [assistant's](Assistant.md) model.

## Privacy

- The bar shows whenever the microphone is open.
- The recording stays on your computer and is deleted when dictation ends.
- Password fields never get dictated text.
- Locking the screen discards whatever is being dictated.
