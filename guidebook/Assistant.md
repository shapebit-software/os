# Assistant

The assistant answers questions and does things on your computer when you ask.
Its model runs on your computer, so what you ask stays there, unless you add
an online model.

It needs the **Gemma 4 E2B** extra, which Welcome turns on where the computer
has enough memory (about 3.5 GB while loaded).

## Asking

- **Type**: tap Super, or click the assistant's button. Tap again to close.
- **Speak**: hold Super or the button, speak, and let go. The answer shows
  beside the button. Speaking needs the **Dictation** extra.
- **Voice mode**: double-tap Super, or right-click the button. It listens
  until you say "stop", tap Super, or are silent for a minute, and answers
  aloud (with the **Spoken answers** extra; turn this off in Settings >
  Assistant). Voice mode conversations are never saved.

A conversation shows as **bubbles** by the button, in a **panel** on the
right of the screen (push the pointer against the screen's right edge), or in
the panel **docked**, where windows make room for it. Moving between them
keeps the conversation.

## What it sees

Only what you send: the selected text, and the name and title of the window
you're in. Both show before you send, and you can remove either. It never
sees the clipboard or what's in your files.

It can look at more only with your permission: which windows are open, and
pictures of the screen. Pictures need the **Screen understanding** extra
(Settings > Assistant).

## What it does

Every action shows as a card:

- **Looking up** your computer's status, your apps, or files by name happens
  at once.
- **Seeing** your screen or **looking things up on the web** asks first.
  Web pages are read without running their scripts, and only public
  addresses are reached.
- **Changes**, such as volume, Wi-Fi, opening apps, or moving windows, ask
  first: once, always, or not at all. A change can be undone from its card.
- **Shutting down, restarting, and installing or removing apps** ask every
  time.

In voice mode you can say "yes" to a change, and "no" to anything. Seeing the
screen and the web always need a click.

**Settings > Assistant > Permissions** shows what you've always allowed, and
the activity log of what it did.

## Online models

Add online models in **Settings > Assistant > Models**, or from the model's
name in the assistant:

- **OpenAI**: **Continue with ChatGPT** uses your ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan,
  or use an OpenAI API key.
- **Claude** and **Gemini**: an API key, billed by the provider. Their
  providers don't allow signing in with a plan.

An online model receives what you ask, and what the assistant reads for it,
under your account with that provider. Keys stay in your keyring.

## Skills

Skills are instructions the assistant reads when a request needs them, such
as the built-in **writing** skill for letters, emails, and articles. Add your
own in **Settings > Assistant > Skills and tools**.

## Good to know

- Answers come from an AI and can be wrong.
- Locking the screen hides the assistant.
- Conversations stay on your computer until you delete them. A conversation
  left for half an hour ends, and the next request starts a new one.
- **Settings > Assistant** chooses when the models load: right after sign-in,
  so answers come at once, or when first used, to save memory.
