# Updates

ShapeBit updates itself. You don't have to do anything.

- Shortly after the computer starts, and then every few hours, ShapeBit
  checks for a new release, but not on a metered connection. **Settings >
  Updates > Check now** checks at once.
- A new release downloads in the background. When it's ready, a notification
  says so, and **Restart** in the system menu reads **Restart to update**.
- ShapeBit never restarts by itself, and never ends your session for an
  update. The next time you restart or shut down, the new release is
  installed.
- Your files, settings, and apps carry over. The previous release stays
  installed.

Every release is tested by installing the previous one and updating it,
before it reaches you. A release with a problem is withdrawn, and computers
go back to the release before it at their next update.

Apps from Settings > Apps update separately, every day ([Apps](Apps.md)).
