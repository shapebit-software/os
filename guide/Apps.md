# Apps

## Built-in apps

These come with ShapeBit, and update with it:

- **Settings**: the computer, the desktop, your account, and installing apps.
- **Files**: your files, archives, and thumbnails.
- **Terminal**: the command line, on the system and in your
  [containers](Containers.md).

## Recommended apps

At your first sign-in, Welcome offers these apps, all turned on; switch off
any you don't want:

- **Text Editor**, **Image Viewer**, **Document Viewer**, **Video Player**,
  **Audio Player**, and **Calculator**.

They open their file types by default.

## Installing apps

**Settings > Apps** is the app store, with apps from
[Flathub](https://flathub.org): search, browse by category, or see what's
installed. Verified apps come first. Searches run on your computer, so what
you look for isn't sent anywhere.

Apps install into your own account. Removing one asks first, and keeps the
app's data unless you choose to delete it. Built-in apps can't be removed.

Apps update every day in the background; a running app gets its update the
next time it opens.

## Browsers

Welcome offers a default browser; you don't have to pick one:

| Browser | Comes from |
|---|---|
| Google Chrome | Flathub, which downloads Google's own build and checks it |
| Chromium | Fedora |
| Firefox | Flathub |
| GNOME Web | Flathub |

The browser you choose installs into your account and becomes your default.
