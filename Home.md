# ShapeBit Operating System

## A personal operating system that gets out of your way

ShapeBit OS is being designed for people who want a modern, secure, and stable
computer without turning system maintenance into another job. Routine updates,
window management, and desktop organization should stay out of the way, leaving
you free to focus on your work and finish what you start.

Everyday use is intended to feel simple, with complexity hidden until it is
wanted. ShapeBit is intended to remain deeply customizable for people who need
more control, while exploring new ways of working through thoughtfully
integrated AI support, alternative input methods, and other emerging
technologies.

ShapeBit is [source-available](License.md), but it is not open-source or free
software. It will remain free of charge for individual users.

ShapeBit OS is a Linux operating system based on Fedora.
